History of Mohlala`s Workshop

    
                 History - 
                The business was founded in 2015 by MR 
                Mohlala Leeto after he finished
                his diploma in motor mechanics engineering 
                from Tshwane University of Technology. 
                He started by fixing  vehicles at his 
                place as a way to make a living. 
                He specialized by fixing any mechanical 
                problem a vehicle can give you. 

                He later came up with an idea to build a 
                workshop along with his brother MR Mohlala Molefe
                after he finished his bachelor's degree in mechatronics 
                engineering from Stellenbosch University in 2018.
                His brother specializes in Auto Electrical. They both built a 
                workshop in 2020, and their workshop started operating in 2021.

Mission and Vision of the workshop

                  -The mission of building this business 
                   was to make a living from the 
                   skills they are at tertiary. 
                   To train and employ people in their community. 

                - The version is that they see the workshop
                  minimizing the unemployment rate in their 
                  community as they train and then employ the 
                  people in their community.
